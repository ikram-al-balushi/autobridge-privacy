Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

AutoBridge ("we", "our", or "the app") is a dealer-to-dealer car marketplace mobile application developed by Muhammad Ikram Bin Abdul Hadi. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect the following information:

  • Name — your full name
  • Phone number — used for login and contact purposes (Call/WhatsApp)
  • Email address — used for login and account-related notifications
  • Showroom name — your dealership name
  • City — your dealership location
  • Password — stored securely in hashed form (we cannot see your password)

2. Vehicle Listing Data

When you post a vehicle listing, we collect:

  • Vehicle details (make, model, year, price, mileage, and other specifications)
  • Vehicle photos uploaded by you

Vehicle photos are compressed and stored on Cloudflare R2 (a cloud storage service) for fast and reliable delivery within the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your dealer account
  • Display your vehicle listings to other registered dealers
  • Enable other dealers to contact you via Call or WhatsApp
  • Send account-related emails (signup confirmation, account approval)
  • Authenticate your identity when you log in

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Your dealer name, showroom name, and phone number are visible to other registered dealers when they view your vehicle listings. This is necessary for the app to function as a dealer-to-dealer marketplace.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Your account data is stored on secure servers
  • Passwords are hashed and cannot be viewed by anyone, including us
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage
  • Vehicle photos are stored on Cloudflare R2 cloud storage
  • All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS encryption

6. Account Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data. You can do this in two ways:

  • In-App: Go to the Profile tab, tap "Delete Account", and confirm. Your deletion request will be submitted and processed by our team.
  • Email: Send a request to autobridge.connect@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your dealer profile (name, phone, email, showroom name, city)
  • All your vehicle listings
  • All vehicle photos uploaded by you (removed from cloud storage)
  • Your authentication token

You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

7. Data Retention

  • Your account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Vehicle listings can be deleted by you at any time
  • Vehicles marked as sold are automatically removed after 7 days
  • If you request account deletion, all your data is permanently removed (see Section 6)

8. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare R2 — for storing vehicle photos
  • WhatsApp — when you tap the WhatsApp button, it opens the WhatsApp app on your device. We do not access your WhatsApp data.
  • Phone dialer — when you tap the Call button, it opens your phone's dialer. We do not access your call logs.

9. Permissions

The app may request the following device permissions:

  • Camera / Photo Library — to take or select photos for vehicle listings
  • Internet — to communicate with our servers

10. Children's Privacy

AutoBridge is intended for use by car dealers and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of your data, please contact us at:
